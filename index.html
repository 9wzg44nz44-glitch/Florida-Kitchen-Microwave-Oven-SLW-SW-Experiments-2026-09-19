1 · What we’re doing so far

A consumer microwave oven is already a strong Faraday cage at 2.45 GHz (TEM leakage is tightly regulated). Extended Electrodynamics / Dr. Hively-style bookkeeping asks whether any non-TEM component (Scalar Longitudinal Wave SLW or Scalar Wave SW) could still be detectable outside or through soft Faraday cloth when the oven is on — or whether what we see is ordinary TEM leakage, multipath, or instrument artifact.

Kitchen work so far is an effective mockup / first listen: TinySA Ultras, soft Faraday-cloth bag sealed by multi-wrap + kitchen clips, oven on/off baselines, then probe swaps inside the cloth (H10 magnetic → Tekbox E5 electric). It maps to the longer lab proposal (Hively microwave-oven Faraday-cage methods) and also informs Experiment A packing (sphere / mesh enclosure discipline).

Dr. Hively claim checklist — required before any SLW / SW claim

From Dr. Hively’s reply 20 Sep 2026 · 2:20 AM ET (subject: RE: Watch the videos in the link of my last email (ideas protected under NDA)). He opened with: “You are not detecting the scalar wave.” Warm skepticism: we are on the right track, but this checklist is not finished.

His exact requirements to definitively show detection of an SLW:

  1. zero B-field (use a magnetic dipole antenna);
  2. a scalar field (use an array of high-magnetic-hysteresis, half-wave-length, linear dipoles);
  3. an electric field along the direction of propagation (longitudinal not transverse) — use an array of half-wavelength, linear, resistive dipoles;
  4. 1/r² attenuation in the far field over at least 20 wavelengths, preferably >50 wavelengths.

Also: for the dipoles, rotate the dipole like Monstein/Wesley did to show directionality. See Table 1 in the 2019 Hively/Loebl paper.

Do not paraphrase as “no E-field inside the cage.” Dr. Hively requires showing a longitudinal E along the propagation direction (not transverse), plus zero B, scalar-field dipoles, and far-field 1/r² — not the absence of E. Soft-cloth seal quality, multipath, probe orientation, and TEM leakage vs hypothesized longitudinal content remain open. A peak on a TinySA is not “proven SLW.”
On the right track. H10 B-null listens and today’s E5 configuration work are useful progress — not a defeat, and not a claim. Working through his list before any SLW language.

Bifilar hybrid coil (TinySA #2)

Two opposing-wind single-wire pancake coils, electrically joined at the center (coil end of the wire), laid on top of each other so the currents are opposing. Intent: the magnetic fields largely cancel (TEM / loop-like B response suppressed), leaving a detector more sensitive to hypothesized longitudinal / scalar-ish coupling than a plain monopole — still HYP, not a proof.

Cloth seal (important)

Edges are not left open for the real runs: three wraps of fabric-on-fabric, held shut with kitchen thumb/finger clips (same family as cereal-bag clips). Seal first; grounding is optional.
